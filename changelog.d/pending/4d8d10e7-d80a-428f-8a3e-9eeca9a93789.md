### Fixed

- **A calculator destination becomes a route stop only once the map pins it.** Until then the form says the place is being found. Where the search gives each place its state, the state wins over a village of the same name. If no place matches, the pin fails, or it takes eight seconds, the name waits in the stop box. The traveller then edits the name to see suggestions. Once the traveller types in the stop box or adds a stop, a late result adds nothing.
