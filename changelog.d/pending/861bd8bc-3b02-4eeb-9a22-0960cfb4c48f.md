### Changed

- **Every visible label starts with a capital letter.** More than 80 labels, chips, badges and captions across the app now start with a capital. This includes the My trips hero counts. Units after numbers and example values stay as they are.
- **A new test stops lowercase labels from coming back.** `tests/ui-label-capitals.test.ts` scans all of `src/`. It fails on a new lowercase label and keeps a short, reasoned list of exceptions.
