### Changed

- **Group input now reads as one Decide together page.** Decisions, stop ideas and activity sit in three columns under a pill bar. The pill counts show only after their read has settled. Each option shows its vote share, its voters and its detail. The engine marks its best fit when it picks one. A stop idea is a row. It opens to vote, comment, add to the timeline or decline. A row that needs your vote says so in words. Resolved decisions show their context. Activity shows the newest four entries first. The three sections are named regions with a visible focus ring. (#972)
- **The Group input tab now moves like the Budget tab.** The header, the pill bar and the cards rise in one after another, in reading order. The rows inside a card trail the card. The vote bars and the crew preference bars grow from the left when you can see them. Their percentage labels fade in after them. An idea that you add or decline fades out, and so does a decision that moves to decided. The rest of the list then glides into the gap. The idea panel, the comment field and the add-option field open and close smoothly. So do the activity list and the quiz. The "Needs your vote" and "Best fit" chips pop in. Every effect stops when the reader asks for reduced motion. (#972)
