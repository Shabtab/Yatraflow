### Fixed

- **Link previews now load Wikimedia covers from our own domain.** A publication whose cover is a Commons upload used to advertise the Wikimedia address. Facebook's crawler gets 403 there, so its preview had no image. Every other crawler gets the 1.3 MB original. The card now points at `/api/cover`. That function asks the MediaWiki API for one known width. It fetches the thumb once and caches the bytes for 30 days. A failure sends the brand card. A cover in our Supabase bucket keeps its own address.
