### Added

- **A trip-cost calculator hands its estimate into the app.** The destination calculators link to `/new?ref=calculator&…` with the days, people, mode, bed tier and per-person budget. A signed-out visitor goes to sign-up, and their estimate is stashed first. The auth page says what is waiting. The create form pre-fills every stated field. The traveller only says where they start. A missing or unusable value is dropped, never defaulted.

### Changed

- **`calculator` joins the share-attribution vocabulary.** The app and `api/i.js` accept the value. The new `20261125_calculator_share_source.sql` widens both CHECK constraints to match. A trip created from a calculator page can now be traced to that page.
