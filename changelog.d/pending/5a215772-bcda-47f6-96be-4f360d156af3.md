### Changed

- **The Map tab has four lenses: Drive, Stay, Gap and Fix, as in the approved mockups.** The separate Plan lens is gone. In the day list, an open part such as lunch now opens the Gap panel with its picks. The Gap panel header holds Fill the day and a Day plan button. Day plan opens the full list of parts, with search, votes, the pinned rest and the Shape view. Its day chips, its legend and two of its three counts are gone. The day count shows once, in the Day plan header. A rain chance of 40% or more shows there too.
- **Day plan rows use labels, not sentences.** An empty part reads "Open · 2 picks". A part the app adds reads "Added by the app". A past habit reads "Usual detour · +8 min".
