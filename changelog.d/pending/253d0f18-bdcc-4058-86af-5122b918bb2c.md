### Added

- **The log in and sign up page shows a cover with the product line.** On a desktop screen, a picture of a winding hill road fills the left column. A glass panel on it shows the locked main line "Plan Indian trips together — not in a chaotic group chat." and the support line "Every stop, hour and rupee in one plan your group can actually follow." The UI em dash scan exempts that exact main line and no other text. The panel also has the chips "Free to plan", "Works offline" and "Made for India". On a phone, a 150 px strip of the same picture sits above the form. The dark theme shows a night version of the picture. The picture is decoration only. (#1010)
- **The sign-up password hint counts the missing characters as you type.** A short password gets the hint "At least 8 characters, 3 more to go" in the error colour. At 8 characters the hint goes back to plain text. (#1010)
- **The log in form shows an invite line only when the return route is an invite route.** The line is "Joining with an invite? Log in and you land straight in the trip." A plain log in does not show it. (#1010)

### Changed

- **A wrong email or password now gets a plain message.** The message is "That email and password don't match. Try again, or mail support to reset." The page no longer shows the provider text "Invalid login credentials". A network failure now reads "We can't reach the server. Check your connection and try again." That line does not name the provider or a browser rule. The raw error text goes to the browser console. The message for a build without a backend does not change. The error shows in a tinted banner. (#1010)
- **The log in and sign up form has new field, button and tab styles.** The email and password fields have a leading icon and a teal focus ring. The submit button is 46 px high and shows a spinning icon while it works. The mode tabs sit on a sunken track with a raised pill, and the pill still glides between tabs. The extra sign-up fields fade and move as the form opens and closes. The mode labels cross-fade. The cover settles in. Every new motion uses the motion tokens and stops when the user asks for reduced motion. (#1010)
