# Illustration style

Short style brief for the papercut scenes. The build and the file rules are in
[`docs/ART-PIPELINE.md`](../../docs/ART-PIPELINE.md). The design rule is in
[`DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md#illustration).

## Look

- Layered cut paper. Five to seven flat layers, from the far sky to the near foreground.
- Soft drop shadows on each layer, lit from the upper left. No outlines.
- Flat colour inside each layer, with a fine paper grain.
- Gentle morning or golden-hour light. No midday glare.
- Frontal, raised eye level. No fish-eye view.

## Palette

Use the tokens in [`DESIGN_TOKENS.md`](../../DESIGN_TOKENS.md). Keep every sky mid-value, so the
image reads in both themes. No region is near-black.

## Hard rules

- No text, letters, digits, signage, logos, flags or watermarks.
- No faces and no real people. A faceless silhouette is allowed when a scene needs scale.
- No maps and no outlines of India or of any state.
- No religious figures or deities. Places of worship appear only as distant architecture.
- No readable brands, livery or emblems.
- No disaster, police, military or protest imagery.

## Layout

- The horizon sits between 40% and 62% of the height from the top.
- The main subject sits in the central 60% of the width.
- Keep the top-left and top-right corners quiet, because the card tags sit there.
- Keep the bottom 22% calm, because the card captions sit there.
- Keep important detail at least 4% away from every edge.
