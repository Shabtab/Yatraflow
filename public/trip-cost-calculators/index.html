Why the range, not one number

One number would pretend to know things nobody knows yet: what your room actually costs in the week you travel, what the pump price is that month, and how far you really drive. Each calculator uses the cheaper figure for every line at the low end and the dearer one at the high end, so your real spend has a good chance of landing between them. Every line is editable, and every page's link keeps your numbers so you can send it to the group.

When the group is ready, each calculator hands your route, days, party size and budget straight into a YatraFlow plan, so nobody retypes anything.
