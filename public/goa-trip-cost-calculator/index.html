Questions about Goa trip costs

How much does a 4-day Goa trip cost for 4 friends?

With the default settings here, four people on a 4-day North Goa trip in peak season, arriving by train, sharing two scooties and two guesthouse rooms and eating at shacks, the calculator lands near ₹6,900 to ₹12,700 per person. That is ₹27,400 to ₹50,600 for the group. Flights instead of the train, a hotel instead of a guesthouse, or a packed New Year week each move the top of that range up.

What is the Goa budget per person from Bengaluru, Mumbai or Delhi?

The travel line changes most with your city. Mumbai and Pune are the cheapest to reach by train, Bengaluru is a longer but still manageable overnight run, and Delhi usually means a flight. Set the Getting there option to match how you plan to arrive and the calculator shows the difference immediately. The rest of the trip, stay, food, and getting around, costs about the same wherever you start.

Do I need a permit to visit Goa?

No. Goa is an ordinary domestic trip with no permits and no registration fees. That is why this calculator has no permit line, unlike Ladakh or Meghalaya. What it does count is the optional nightlife and water-sports buffer, which is the line most groups forget.

Is a scooty or a car better for a group in Goa?

A scooty is cheaper and easier to park, at about ₹400 to ₹600 a day, and one between two people covers a group of four for ₹800 to ₹1,200 a day. A self-drive car costs about ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 a day and its fuel is extra, but it is better in the rain and for the longer north-to-south drive. Switch the Getting around option above and compare.

What are the best months to visit Goa, and how does season change the price?

November to February is the dry, busy season, and December plus the first week of January is the most expensive part of it. Rooms roughly double against the monsoon months, and flights spike around 31 December. Late October and March are the value weeks with the same weather. The ranges here are a normal peak-season week, not New Year week.
