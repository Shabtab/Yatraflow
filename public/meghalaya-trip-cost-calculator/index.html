Questions about Meghalaya trip costs

How much does a private cab cost in Meghalaya for 5 days?

An Innova or Bolero-class SUV with a driver runs about ₹3,000 to ₹4,500 a day for the Guwahati–Shillong–Cherrapunji–Dawki loop, and five days is the usual quote. That is ₹15,000 to ₹22,500 for the car, split across your group, so four people pay roughly ₹3,750 to ₹5,600 each for transport alone. The driver's food and stay are usually settled separately at the end of the trip, and are not in this figure.

What is the Meghalaya budget per person for 4 friends?

With the default settings here, four people on the 5-day classic circuit in a private SUV with a driver, staying in homestays and eating locally, the calculator lands near ₹9,700 to ₹16,600 per person. That is ₹38,600 to ₹66,500 for the group. Shared cabs bring transport down sharply and add waiting time; rented bikes sit in between and are the most fun in the dry months.

Do I need a permit or registration for Meghalaya?

Meghalaya asks visitors to register, and the fee is small: roughly ₹100 to ₹200 a person. It is counted as its own line here because groups forget it. Some individual sights, such as the Double Decker root bridge and Dawki boating, charge their own fees and are covered by the entry line. Nothing else needs a permit for Indian nationals. Check the state tourism site for the current rule before you travel, because it changes.

Should we rent bikes or take a cab in Meghalaya?

Bikes are cheaper on paper, about ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 a bike a day plus fuel, and one bike between two people works well for a group of four. But the roads climb hard and the rain arrives fast, and some Shillong roads restrict bike taxis. A cab with a driver costs more and removes the weather risk. Switch the Getting around option above and compare both totals rather than the daily rate.

What are the best months to visit Meghalaya?

October to April is the dry season and the safe window. Cherrapunji and Mawsynram are the wettest places in India, and June to September brings the waterfalls at their fullest along with slides, washed-out roads and trails that close without notice. The ranges here are a dry-season week. If you go in the monsoon, add to the buffer and expect to drop a day.
