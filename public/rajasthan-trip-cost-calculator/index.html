Questions about Rajasthan trip costs

How much does a 7-day Rajasthan trip cost for 4 people?

With the default settings here, four people on the full Jaipur–Jodhpur–Jaisalmer–Udaipur circuit in a haveli with local thalis, driving their own car, the calculator lands near ₹15,800 to ₹25,800 per person. That is ₹63,200 to ₹1,03,200 for the group. A car with a driver adds to that, and it takes the driving fatigue out. Two people instead of four pushes the per-person figure up because the car and the rooms are shared between fewer people.

What costs do groups usually forget in Rajasthan?

Fort and monument tickets are the big one: they are cheap individually and add up to ₹700 to ₹1,600 a person over the circuit, and camera fees and parking are extra. Highway tolls for a self-drive car are another ₹1,000 to ₹2,200 for the whole car. The desert camp night is India's most common unplanned splurge. All three have their own line here, so you can see them before you go rather than after.

Is it cheaper to drive, take a car with a driver, or go by train?

Driving your own car is the cheapest on paper, because you pay for fuel and tolls and nothing else. A car with a driver costs the most, but it includes fuel and tolls and removes 2,000 km of driving from your group. The train looks cheap until you add the local cabs at both ends, which is why the train option here prices them together. Switch the Getting around option and compare the totals, not the headline rate.

Do I need a permit for Rajasthan?

No. There are no permits or tourism registrations for a domestic Rajasthan circuit. You pay monument entry tickets, and that is the only government fee involved. Unlike Ladakh or Meghalaya, nothing here needs an online registration before you travel.

What are the best months to visit Rajasthan, and how does season change the price?

November to February is the season. Summer crosses 45°C in Jaisalmer and Jodhpur, so nobody drives the circuit then. December and January are the busiest weeks, and desert camps charge their highest rates. October and early March are the value weeks with the same monuments and fewer people. The ranges here are a normal winter week.
